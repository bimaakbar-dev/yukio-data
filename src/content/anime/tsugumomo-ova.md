This OVA tells six short side stories to the main series titled "Boku no Tsukumogami-sama", "Sentou nite", "Boku no Goshujin-sama", "Kokuyou no Ongaeshi", "Saigo no Toride", and "Erohon Kaigi". The OVA was bundled with the special edition of the 24th manga volume.
