The anime adaption of the four-panel manga "Ai Mai Mi".

The story follows girls in a manga club &mdash;Ai, Mai, Mii, and Ponoka-sempai&mdash;who might be fighting evil invaders threatening Earth, facing off against rivals in tournaments, and dealing with other absurd situations when they are not drawing manga.
