A scientist couple has been arrested for conducting illegal studies, allegedly for performing genetic modification on five of their children. The youngest child, Dannis, is the only one who grew up as a normal human, and has become the only family member who has to tend to rest of the members and to try to find a medicine to turn them into normal humans again.

Dog human, plant gene, spider web gene, as well as mind reading, how will these family members who grew up in the laboratory survive in the outside world?!
