As spring arrives, goblin activity increases, which is great news for Goblin Slayer, who will do anything to exterminate these vile creatures. While taking on as many goblin slaying quests as possible, he attracts the attention of an ill-tempered novice wizard who asks for his guidance. Although reluctant at first, Goblin Slayer and his fellow adventurers are soon tasked with instructing the boy and several other rookies. With goblins continuing to cause harm nearby, Goblin Slayer will show no mercy to these monsters and ensure that everyone else is prepared.
