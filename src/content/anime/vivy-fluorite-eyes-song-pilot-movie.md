Pilot of the Vivy -Fluorite Eye’s Song- anime that was produced before the main story was made. After the show had fully aired, it was first unveiled on August 21, 2021 in WIT STUDIO's 10th Anniversary Online Talk Show #3 with featured guest and Vivy character designer loundraw. The pilot was later separately published on WIT Studio's official YouTube channel on August 24, 2021.
