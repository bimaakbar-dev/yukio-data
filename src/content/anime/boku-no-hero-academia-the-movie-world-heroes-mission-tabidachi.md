Animation included with the PLUS ULTRA version of the Boku no Hero Academia THE MOVIE: World Heroes' Mission Blu-ray/DVD release. The OVA is an adaptation of the "Hawks: SOOTHE" one-shot from the Vol. World Heroes booklet that was given out during the movie's theatrical run. 
 

Note: This animation received an advance limited screening run alongside the World Heroes' Mission movie in theaters from January 22 to 27th in Japan.
