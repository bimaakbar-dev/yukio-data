The second season of Boushoku no Berserk.

The Gluttony skill. In a world where magical skills shape your destiny, Fate has awakened the unparalleled power of Gluttony—the ability to devour the skills and stats of anyone he kills. After surviving countless deadly battles alongside Greed, his black sword and trusted companion, Fate finally returns to the royal capital. Once one of the have-nots, shunned and looked down upon, Fate has never forgotten what it was like to live at the very bottom. Now, he takes a new step forward as a Holy Knight. But an even greater battle awaits him—one that will lead him closer to the truth behind their world. Now, Fate’s destiny begins to move once more.
